    BRANDEN CHI

    Art Director
    he/him

    Hey! I'm Branden Chi, an Art Director based in New York by way of small-town Delaware. I got my start with video games and computers, which naturally segued into digital design and eventually advertising and art direction. I'm driven by that "damn, I wish I'd thought of that" feeling — and I'd love nothing more than to make something that inspires someone else to create. Outside of work, I'm usually cooking, playing guitar (badly), or starting a new hobby I probably won't finish.
