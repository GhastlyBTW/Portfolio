What do you think it's like working at Mother?

I imagine Mother as a mix between a corporate landscape and a playground for adults. Really, it's a bunch of adults trying not to be adults, because honestly, who actually wants to be one?

In the office, I picture an open floor plan where everyone lowkey gets along, but we still gotta keep it professional. From home, the vibe is laid back. Meetings are super chill and friendly, and there are probably a couple of questionable jokes in Slack from time to time.

It's not all fun and games, though. I'm sure there's a good balance between making fun, wacky stuff and some mind-numbing mundane tasks. There are probably a lot of late nights and corporate slop bowls too, but I'll never hate on Chipotle.

The earliest ad I can remember

Rocket Fishing Rod

This is the earliest ad I can remember. It was an ad for a fishing rod my dad bought me when I was eight years old. I hadn't thought about this thing in a while, so it's fun to look back on it. This fishing rod meant a lot to me as a kid. My dad loved fishing and the outdoors, so this was his way of convincing me to come with him.

What I'm most proud of

I'm most proud of my family and my heritage. I'm so blessed to have an amazing family. My older sister is my role model. She's a creative director, and she's the reason I know I can be successful in the creative field. Everyone says you can't, that it's not a viable path, but she's showing everyone that you can. My mom is an incredible woman who has provided so much for me. My little sister is such an inspiration and one of my best friends. My dad was always so much fun to be around, and he's where I got a lot of my personality. He loved cooking and he loved family, and I'm so proud to have such a great family around me.

I'm also proud of my Korean and Italian heritage. I grew up pretty disconnected from my culture, but I try my best to connect with it as much as I can, and to really learn about it and embrace it.

What I'm least proud of

My ego. Sometimes it gets the better of me and stops me from seeing things in a different light, whether in my personal life or my career. I think having an ego can be both good and bad. On one hand, I'm not one to back down from an idea. When I believe in something, I really believe in it. But it can also blind me to outside advice and reason.
