The earliest ad I can remember

Rocket Fishing Rod

This is the earliest ad I can remember. It was an ad for a fishing rod my dad bought me when I was eight years old. I hadn't thought about this thing in a while, so it's fun to look back on it. This fishing rod meant a lot to me as a kid. My dad loved fishing and the outdoors, so this was his way of convincing me to come with him.
